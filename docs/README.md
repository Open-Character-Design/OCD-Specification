# Open Character Design Specification (OCD) – Mission & Overview

## 📍 Summary

The **Open Character Design Specification (OCD)** is an open, interoperable standard for defining, exchanging, and interpreting characters across any medium or technology. From video games and novels to film, theater, comics, AI systems, and beyond, OCD provides a structured, portable blueprint that captures every aspect of a character in a way that's both human-authorable and machine-readable.

---

## 🌐 Comprehensive Overview

### What Is OCD?

The **Open Character Design Specification (OCD)** is a structured, extensible data model for describing characters ,  fictional or real, physical or digital, human or otherwise. It defines a unified schema that captures the full spectrum of a character’s identity, appearance, psychology, backstory, relationships, abilities, behaviors, and meta-context.

The goal is simple but ambitious: **to create a common language for character data that can flow seamlessly between creative, technical, and AI-driven systems.** By doing so, OCD allows writers, designers, developers, and researchers to work from the same source of truth ,  no matter what toolchain, platform, or medium they use.

---

### Why It Exists

Today, character information is fragmented and inconsistent. Game studios have proprietary schemas, screenwriters use prose bibles, AI models rely on free-form prompts, and performance notes live in scattered documents. This fragmentation creates friction, duplication, and incompatibility.

OCD solves this by establishing a **standardized character model** that works across all contexts. It ensures that a character written in a screenplay can power a dialogue system in a video game, inform an AI persona, or become a metadata-rich record in an archive ,  all without rework or reinterpretation.

---

### Core Design Principles

1. **Universality** – Represent *any* character type across *any* medium: game NPCs, mythological gods, comic villains, real-world historical figures, AI chat agents, and more.
2. **Composability** – Break down a character into modular components: identity, appearance, metaphysical traits, personality, relationships, narrative role, etc.
3. **Interoperability** – Make definitions portable across authoring tools, engines, pipelines, and AI frameworks.
4. **Extensibility** – Support project- or domain-specific extensions (`x-*`) without breaking compatibility.
5. **Human + Machine Symbiosis** – Be easy to write and read by humans (via YAML or JSON) while also being strictly structured for validation and automation.
6. **Safety & Responsibility** – Include built-in fields for audience targeting, content ratings, and representation considerations.
7. **Lifecycle Awareness** – Support dynamic state changes (mood, health, location, arc progression) as well as static biography.

### What OCD Enables

* **Writers & Worldbuilders:** A portable standard for character definitions that plug directly into story bibles, scripts, and generative tools.
* **Game Developers:** A canonical format for NPC data that can drive AI, dialogue, simulation, and narrative progression.
* **AI Systems:** Rich, structured personas that guide behavior, memory, conversation, and emergent storytelling.
* **Filmmakers & Performers:** Unified references for characterization, casting, continuity, and performance direction.
* **Researchers & Archivists:** Machine-readable records for analyzing character networks, evolution, and cultural impact.

---

### The Broader Vision

OCD is more than just a specification ,  it’s an **interface between creativity and computation**. By standardizing how characters are described and exchanged, it enables new kinds of interoperability between storytelling, software, and AI. Whether you’re building a game, writing a novel, training a large language model, or running a virtual production, OCD provides the foundation for representing characters in a way that is portable, precise, and future-proof.
