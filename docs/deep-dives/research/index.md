# Research Papers 📚

Academic research and theoretical foundations of character modeling, interoperability, and cross-media character representation.

## Overview

This section contains peer-reviewed research, academic papers, and theoretical analysis that underpin the Open Character Design Specification. These documents provide the scientific and academic foundation for OCD's design decisions and validate its approach to character modeling.

## Research Areas

### Character Modeling Theory
- Psychological foundations of character representation
- Cognitive models of character perception and interaction
- Cross-cultural character archetype analysis
- Narrative theory and character development

### AI and Machine Learning
- Persona modeling for large language models
- Character behavior prediction and simulation
- Training data optimization for character-based AI
- Ethical considerations in AI character representation

### Interoperability and Standards
- Cross-platform character data exchange
- Schema evolution and backward compatibility
- Performance analysis of character data structures
- Security and privacy in character data sharing

### Cross-Media Analysis
- Character consistency across different media
- Adaptation strategies for character portability
- Audience reception and character recognition studies
- Cultural impact of standardized character representation

## Current Research Papers

### [Common Character Definition Fields Across All Mediums](common-character-fields.md)
**Authors**: OCD Research Team  
**Date**: 2024  
**Abstract**: Comprehensive analysis of universal character attributes that appear consistently across all storytelling mediums—literature, film, television, games, and interactive media. This research identifies the core fields that define character identity and behavior regardless of medium, forming the theoretical foundation for OCD's core validation fields.

**Key Findings**:
- Identification of 6 core character definition categories
- Cross-media consistency validation across all storytelling mediums
- Psychological foundations for character recognition and consistency
- Implementation guidelines for AI and cross-platform systems

**Impact**: This research directly informs the OCD core schema design and validation criteria, ensuring interoperability and consistency across diverse applications.

## Submission Guidelines

We welcome research contributions that advance the field of character modeling and interoperability. Research papers should:

- Follow academic standards for methodology and citation
- Include empirical data or theoretical analysis
- Be relevant to character modeling, AI, or interoperability
- Undergo peer review before publication

For submission guidelines, see our [Contributing Guide](../../governance/contributing-to-spec.md).
